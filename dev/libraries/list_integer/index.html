MAGE.listinteger_iscond.bundle_listinteger_iscond — Constant
bundle_listinteger_iscond

Comparisons producing integer masks (1/0), the raw material for mask-based subsetting.

Against zero: is_sup_0, is_eq_0, is_less_0. Against a value: is_eq_to, greater_than_broadcast, less_than_broadcast, eq_broadcast. Between neighbours or lists: is_more_than_right, is_more_eq_than_right, is_eq_to_prev, compare_two_vectors. Over pairs: compare_tuple_a_gr_b, compare_tuple_a_eq_b, compare_tuple_a_less_b. Positional and mask algebra: even_indices_mask, odd_indices_mask, mask, inverse_mask.
